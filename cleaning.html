Questions actual cleaning owners ask us.

I already use ZenMaid, Jobber, or Launch27. Why switch?

Those tools are fine for scheduling and basic invoicing. Avolv runs the front of your business: an AI receptionist and estimator that reads texts, photos, and calls and drafts quotes in seconds, a real CRM pipeline, marketing and reviews on autopilot, auto-pay, voice-to-invoice from the field. Most owners either keep their scheduler alongside Avolv at first or switch entirely. Side by side, you replace four or five separate apps with one.

How long does setup take?

About 5 business days from "yes" to live. We do it for you: phone number provisioning, A2P SMS registration, website setup, service catalog build, crew accounts, and a 60-minute training call. You don't have to be technical.

What if the AI sends a wrong quote?

It doesn't send. It drafts. Every AI-generated quote sits in your pipeline waiting for you to tap send or edit first. Your service catalog defines the price ranges, so the draft can't go outside your guardrails. Once you trust it, you can turn on auto-send for specific service types.

Will my customers know they're texting a bot?

They know your business answered fast and got them a quote. We don't impersonate humans, but we also don't announce "Hi, I'm a bot" on every text. The AI uses your business voice, signs with your business name, and hands off to you the moment a question gets out of scope.

It depends on your business. One crew or ten, residential or commercial, the platform is the same but how we package it is different. We'd rather give you a real fit on a 15-minute call than a one-size-fits-all sticker. What we tell you upfront: no per-crew fees, no per-seat fees, no setup fees during promotions, no contracts. One monthly price, everything we build, cancel any time.

What does Avolv replace?

Most owners we talk to run four or five separate tools: a scheduling app, QuickBooks, a texting app, an answering service or AI receptionist, a website, and Zapier to glue it together. Yes. Multi-tenant database with row-level isolation, encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted with daily backups. Every Avolv customer's data is walled off from every other customer's. We're working toward SOC 2 Type II for 2026.
