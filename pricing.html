Setup: a one-time fee, quoted before you commit

Setup covers the work that turns a signed-up account into a running business: moving your data off Skimmer or whatever you use now, reconciling accounts against QuickBooks, provisioning your phone number and A2P SMS, building your service catalog, and training your team.

The fee scales with the migration. A small route with clean data starts around $500 to $1,000. A large route with years of history in Skimmer, address issues, and a QuickBooks reconciliation runs higher. Resort Pool Services ran $5,000 because 988 accounts, four systems, and a line-by-line QBO reconciliation is real work.

Pricing questions

The ones that come up on every call.

Why isn't your price published as a fixed number?

Because a fair per-location price on 100 pools is not a fair per-location price on 900. The subscription scales with your service locations so a one-truck operation isn't subsidizing a fleet, and a fleet isn't paying a per-user tax.

Are there per-user or per-truck fees?

No. Add every tech, every dispatcher, and every truck at no additional cost. You pay per service location, not per seat.

What does the setup fee cover?

Moving your data off Skimmer or whatever you use now, reconciling accounts against QuickBooks, provisioning your phone number and A2P SMS, building your service catalog, and training your team. Scoped to your migration and quoted before you commit.

Is there a contract?

No. Every Avolv customer is on a month-to-month subscription. Cancel any time.

How does Avolv compare on total cost?

Most operators arrive paying for four to five separate tools: a scheduler, QuickBooks, a texting app, an AI receptionist or answering service, and Zapier to glue it together. That commonly runs $400 to $700 per month. Avolv usually costs less than that combined bill and replaces the stack.

Do you run specials?

Occasionally. Not on the front page and not as a permanent discount. If you're on the fence, mention it on the call.

Fifteen minutes and I will look at your book and quote you both the monthly and the setup. No obligation.
