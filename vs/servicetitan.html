Questions we get about this comparison

Is ServiceTitan bad software?

No. It is genuinely enterprise-grade and it fits large operations doing complex dispatch. The problem is fit. ServiceTitan has publicly said the platform is not optimized for companies with three or fewer technicians. It is expensive, contract-locked, and built for CSR-driven dispatch of one-off calls rather than route-based recurring service.

Can Avolv migrate my ServiceTitan data?

Migrating out of ServiceTitan is harder than most tools because the export process is not friendly. Some users report needing legal help to retrieve their own data. It is still doable. Talk to me first before signing a cancellation, because timing matters.

Does Avolv have ServiceTitan's marketing features?

Avolv has marketing campaigns, follow-up sequences, review generation on autopilot, and a lead pipeline with AI classification. Not identical to ServiceTitan's full marketing suite, but adequate for most operations without a dedicated marketing team.

Why don't you publish enterprise pricing?

We do. Pricing is public on the pricing page. What we do not do is a $50K implementation, so there is no hidden number.
