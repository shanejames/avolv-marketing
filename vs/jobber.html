Questions we get about this comparison

Is Jobber bad software?

No. Jobber is a genuinely good product with a well-designed workflow. What breaks down for route-based recurring businesses is the per-user pricing model. If you're a 15-tech pool company, you're paying $599/month plus per-user overages, and the AI receptionist is still separate. Avolv is priced on service locations instead, so adding techs doesn't grow your bill.

Can Avolv migrate my Jobber data?

Yes. Full account history, jobs, invoices, and customer records. Recurring schedules preserved. Quoted upfront based on your data size.

Does Avolv have Jobber's client hub?

Yes. Avolv has a customer portal with quote approvals, payments, service history, and appointment booking. Same idea, integrated with the AI receptionist and pipeline.

How does the AI receptionist compare?

Jobber's AI receptionist is a paid add-on (part of higher-tier plans or through partners). Avolv's AI receptionist is included in every plan. It answers calls, books appointments straight onto the route, quotes from a photo or video, and texts you the calls that need a human.

What about QuickBooks Online?

Jobber's QBO sync is basic and users report inconsistencies. Avolv reconciles line by line: invoices generated from the schedule push into QBO, payments post back against the right invoice on their own. Both systems show the same number. Resort's migration reconciled $370,779 to a $580 variance.
