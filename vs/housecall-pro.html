Questions we get about this comparison

Is Housecall Pro bad software?

No, it's a solid product for what it does. The problem operators tell us about is add-on creep. You start at $59, then need QBO ($149 Essentials), then GPS, then Sales Proposals, then a Price Book, then live answering, and suddenly you're at $400+ per month before per-user overages. Avolv bundles all of that into a per-location subscription. Different pricing model, same or better feature coverage.

Can Avolv migrate my HCP data?

Yes. Full accounts, history, recurring schedules. Quoted upfront.

Does Avolv work for HVAC or plumbing?

Avolv is built for route-based recurring services first (pool, cleaning, lawn, pest, pressure washing). HVAC and plumbing are more one-off dispatch, which is Housecall Pro's core. If your business is 100% one-off dispatches with no recurring plans, HCP might be a better fit. If you do any recurring maintenance work, Avolv is worth a look.

How does the AI receptionist compare?

HCP Assist is a paid answering service. Avolv's AI receptionist is included in every plan, answers every call, books straight onto the route, and quotes from photos or videos. If someone texts a picture at 7pm, they get a quote within minutes.

What about the QuickBooks integration?

HCP's QBO sync is a widely reported friction point. Avolv reconciles line by line. Every invoice in the schedule pushes to QBO with the right customer, the right line items, and the right amounts. Payments post back automatically. Both systems show the same number.
