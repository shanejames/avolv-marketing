Questions we get about this comparison

Is Skimmer bad software?

No. Skimmer is a solid product, especially for smaller pool operators who don't need a CRM, marketing tools, or an AI receptionist. If it fits your business, stay on it. Nobody at Avolv is going to pretend Skimmer is broken. What we do differently is bundle the full front-of-business (AI receptionist, marketing, CRM, website) with the pool operations, so you're not paying for four separate tools that don't talk to each other.

Can Avolv migrate my Skimmer data?

Yes. Every account, every service location, every history record, every recurring schedule. Addresses re-geocoded, route zones checked against real vehicle data. Resort moved 988 pools without stopping a single route. Your migration is quoted upfront based on how much data you have and where it lives.

Do I have to give up Skimmer's chemistry features?

No. Avolv has pool chemistry logging with AI meter scanning built in. Some operators keep Skimmer running for a period during migration and turn it off once Avolv has proven out. Most switch entirely.

How does the pricing actually compare?

Skimmer's Scaling Up is $2 per pool per month plus a $99/month AI phone add-on if you want the AI receptionist. Avolv is priced per service location too, but the AI receptionist is included, plus route optimization, QuickBooks reconciliation, marketing campaigns, follow-up sequences, and the website builder. Most operators pay less on Avolv than they were paying on Skimmer plus their scheduling app plus their answering service combined.

What if I want to switch back?

Your data is yours. Avolv doesn't hold it hostage. Every account can be exported at any time, no lawyers required.
