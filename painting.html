Questions actual painting contractors ask us.

I estimate in a spreadsheet and a proposal tool. Why switch?

A spreadsheet does the math and a proposal tool makes it look nice, but you are still measuring, pricing, and typing it all twice. Avolv does the surface takeoff, prices the job against your rates, builds the color schedule, and sends the proposal from one system, so the estimate stops eating your evenings.

How accurate is the surface takeoff?

Avolv sizes the surfaces from a walkthrough and applies your coats, coverage, and spread rates to price the job, and you confirm the numbers before the proposal goes out. It gets a priced, defensible estimate in front of the customer in minutes, with your own rates as the guardrails.

Does it handle both residential and commercial?

Yes. Residential repaints bill with a deposit and a final invoice, and commercial jobs bill in progress draws as phases are completed. Both run through the same takeoff, color schedule, scheduling, and compliance tools.

How does the EPA RRP lead-safe piece work?

Homes built before 1978 are flagged for RRP automatically, and the lead-safe records are captured on the job and kept with a tamper-evident history on the same compliance engine the rest of Avolv runs on. The records exist and hold up when you need to produce them.

How much does Avolv cost?

It depends on the size of your business. We would rather give you a real fit on a 15-minute call than a one-size-fits-all sticker. What we tell you upfront: no per-seat fees, no setup fees during promotions, and no contracts. One monthly price, everything we build, cancel any time.

How long does setup take?

About 5 business days from "yes" to live. We do it for you: phone number provisioning, A2P SMS registration, your labor and paint rates, crew accounts, and a training call. You do not have to be technical.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. Multi-tenant database with row-level isolation, encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted with daily backups. Every Avolv customer's data is walled off from every other customer's. We are working toward SOC 2 Type II for 2026.
