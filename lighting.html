Questions actual lighting installers ask us.

I run on spreadsheets and a scheduling app. Why switch?

Spreadsheets handle the math and a scheduler handles the calendar, but neither one quotes the job from a photo, shows the customer the lights on their own house, or brings last season's customers back. Avolv runs the whole season on one system, from the first photo to the rebook, so you stop stitching the business together by hand.

How accurate is the roofline measurement?

The AI traces the roofline from a photo and estimates the linear feet, and you confirm or adjust it before the quote goes out. It gets you a priced quote in front of the customer in minutes instead of a second trip up the ladder, with a manual adjust for the peaks and gables that need a closer look.

Does it handle permanent roofline and landscape lighting too?

Yes. Holiday installs, permanent roofline systems, and landscape lighting all run through the same quoting, scheduling, inventory, and service tools. A permanent system simply skips the takedown and carries a maintenance plan instead.

How does the rebooking work?

Every install sets a reminder for the following season. Before the calls start coming in, Avolv reaches out to last year's customers to lock in their rebooking, so your easiest sales are handled before the rush.

How much does Avolv cost?

It depends on the size of your business. We would rather give you a real fit on a 15-minute call than a one-size-fits-all sticker. What we tell you upfront: no per-seat fees, no setup fees during promotions, and no contracts. One monthly price, everything we build, cancel any time.

How long does setup take?

About 5 business days from "yes" to live. We do it for you: phone number provisioning, A2P SMS registration, your pricing and product catalog, crew accounts, and a training call. You do not have to be technical.

Is my customer data safe?

Yes. Multi-tenant database with row-level isolation, encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted with daily backups. Every Avolv customer's data is walled off from every other customer's. We are working toward SOC 2 Type II for 2026.
