I already use Skimmer or Pool Service Software. Why switch?

Skimmer is great for the chemical side of pool service. Avolv runs the front of your business: an AI estimator that reads customer videos and drafts quotes in seconds, a real CRM pipeline, marketing automation, voice-to-invoice from the truck, voice notes and reminders. Most customers either keep Skimmer for water chemistry alongside Avolv or switch entirely. Side by side, you replace four or five separate apps with one.

How long does setup take?

About 5 business days from "yes" to live. We do it for you: phone number provisioning, A2P SMS registration, website setup, service catalog import, team accounts, and a 60-minute training call. You don't have to be technical.

What if the AI sends a wrong estimate?

It doesn't send. It drafts. Every AI-generated quote sits in your pipeline waiting for you to tap send or edit first. Your service catalog defines the price ranges, so the draft can't go outside your guardrails. Once you trust it, you can turn on auto-send for specific service types.

Will my customers know they're texting a bot?

They know your business answered fast and got them a quote. We don't impersonate humans, but we also don't announce "Hi, I'm a bot" on every text. The AI uses your business voice, signs messages with your business name, and hands off to you the moment a question gets technical or out of scope.

We'd rather give you a real fit on a 15-minute call than a one-size-fits-all sticker. What we tell you upfront: no per-truck fees, no per-seat fees, no setup fees during promotions, and no contracts. Most owners we talk to are using four or five separate tools: a scheduling app, QuickBooks, a texting app, an AI receptionist or answering service, a website, and Zapier to glue it together. That's typically $400 to $700 per month plus the hours reconciling between them. Avolv replaces all of it in one app, everything talking to everything else. Yes. Multi-tenant database with row-level isolation, encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted with daily backups. Every Avolv customer's data is walled off from every other customer's. We're working toward SOC 2 Type II for 2026.
