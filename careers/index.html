Account Executive

Full-time Remote, or Tampa Bay, FL Base plus commission

You will own the sale from the first conversation to a signed customer. That means reaching pool and home service operators, showing them what Avolv does on their own numbers, and guiding them from demo to decision. You will work the warm pipeline we generate and build your own, with the product team close enough that a customer question gets a real answer the same day.

What you'll do

  • Run discovery calls and live demos with pool and home service company owners.
  • Turn our audits, referrals, and inbound interest into booked demos.
  • Walk operators through pricing, migration, and what switching really looks like for them.
  • Carry deals from the first call to a signed agreement and a clean handoff to onboarding.
  • Bring the field back to us, so what you hear on calls shapes both the product and the pitch.

Who we're looking for

  • You have sold software or a service to small and mid-sized business owners, and you have carried a quota.
  • You are comfortable selling to operators rather than committees, and you can hold a real conversation about someone's business.
  • You explain things plainly and you do not oversell, because our whole brand is built on being straight with people.
  • Bilingual English and Spanish is a plus, since many of the owners and crews we serve speak both.
  • You are based in the United States. Tampa Bay is a bonus, not a requirement.

What we offer

  • Base salary plus uncapped commission, discussed openly on the first call.
  • A product built for the exact people you are selling to, with real customers to point to.
  • Short lines to the founder and the product team, so you are never stuck waiting to get a deal unstuck.
  • Room to grow into a larger role as the company grows.
