Resort Pool Services manages close to a thousand service locations across residential accounts, HOAs, and property management companies. Before Avolv it ran on four systems that each held part of the truth and none that agreed with the others.

The migration

988 accounts moved off Skimmer. Every service location, every history record, every recurring schedule. The part that usually goes wrong is the part nobody mentions in a sales pitch. Addresses that geocode to the wrong side of town. Route zones that no longer match where the trucks drive. Resort had both, so this was not a data dump. Zones were reconciled against real Geotab vehicle data. Downtime for the business: none. The techs ran their routes the entire time.

The books

Resort's real exposure was not scheduling. It was that nobody could prove the invoices matched the work. I reconciled $370,779 of invoicing against QuickBooks Online line by line, compared A/R aging, and aligned invoice numbering so one invoice has one identity in both places. Final variance: $580. Simon can now open two systems and see the same number.
