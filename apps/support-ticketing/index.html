When support lives in a personal inbox and a string of texts, requests get missed and nobody can tell what is still open. Support Ticketing gives those requests a front door and a queue. If you already run a support system, keep it: Avolv can be the front door and pass tickets to your own tool through the API.

How it works

  1. A customer asks for help

    From the in-app help button, an email to your support address, or your public web form.

  2. It becomes a ticket

    Routed into the queue, assigned to a person, and given a status and a priority.

  3. Your team resolves it

    With canned replies, internal notes, and SLA timers, while the customer gets updates along the way.

Who it's for

Operators whose customers need a clear way to ask for help, and who want those requests tracked and owned instead of scattered across texts, inboxes, and voicemails.
