Questions actual estate attorneys ask us.

Does Avolv draft my wills, trusts, and contracts?

No. You draft every instrument and upload the final PDF. Avolv does not write legal documents, and there is no AI drafting of instruments. The responsibility for the law and the document stays entirely with you. Avolv runs the intake, the matter, the signing, and the record around your work.

Is this for litigation or process serving?

No. Avolv's legal module is built for estate and transactional work: wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, and contracts. It is the lifecycle of drafting, review, execution, and filing, not a litigation docket.

Who is responsible for the execution formalities?

You are. The execution rulesets are human-verified and Florida-first, and the attorney accepts responsibility for the formalities before anything is sealed. Avolv records the ceremony and keeps a tamper-evident execution record, but the legal judgment is yours.

Does it handle trust accounting or IOLTA?

No. Avolv handles billing and payments for your matters, but it is not a trust accounting or IOLTA system, and it does not try to be. If you keep trust accounting elsewhere, that stays where it is.

How much does Avolv cost?

It depends on the size of your practice. We would rather give you a real fit on a 15-minute call than a one-size-fits-all sticker. What we tell you upfront: no per-seat fees, no setup fees during promotions, and no contracts. One monthly price, everything we build, cancel any time.

How long does setup take?

About 5 business days from "yes" to live. We do it for you: phone number provisioning, your consultation types and fees, your matter templates, user accounts, and a training call. You do not have to be technical.

Is my client data safe?

Yes. Multi-tenant database with row-level isolation, encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted with daily backups. Every client's matters are walled off from every other firm's. Executed documents are kept with a tamper-evident history, and we are working toward SOC 2 Type II for 2026.
